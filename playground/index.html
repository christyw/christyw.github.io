Look Before the Label

Where did your eye go first?

Pick a detail in your head. Then try looking at the painting through two different labels.

Read it like music

Follow a black line as if it were a phrase in a piece of music. Notice where it speeds up, where another line interrupts, and where the color gives it room.

Read it as a scene

Look for what might be a building, a crowd, or a cannon. Once you think you’ve found one, does the rest of the painting start to look different?

Look again. Does your eye still land in the same place?

One Minute With a Painting

Stay with the painting for one minute. Start wherever your attention lands.
